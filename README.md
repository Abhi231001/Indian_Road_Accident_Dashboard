# Indian_Road_Accident_Dashboard

An interactive data analytics dashboard built using Microsoft Excel to analyze over 500,000 Indian road accident records. The project identifies accident trends, high-risk locations, severity patterns, weather impact, and peak traffic periods to support data-driven road safety decisions.

# Project Description

Road accidents are one of the leading public safety challenges in India. This project transforms raw accident data into meaningful insights using Excel's data cleaning, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and KPI cards.

The dashboard enables users to:

Monitor accident severity across states

Identify cities with the highest accident frequency

Analyze monthly and daily accident trends

Evaluate the impact of weather and road conditions

Detect peak-hour and weekend accident patterns

# Dataset Explanation

The dataset contains 500,000+ accident records with 29 attributes describing the location, time, environment, and severity of each accident.

Identification : Accident ID, Name

Location : City, State, Latitude, Longitude

Date & Time : Date, Month, Year, Hour, Day of Week

Road Information : Road Type, Lanes, Traffic Signal

Weather : Weather, Visibility, Temperature

Traffic : Traffic Density, Peak Hour

Accident Details : Cause, Severity, Vehicles Involved, Casualties

Additional : Weekend, Festival, Risk Score

# Data Cleaning Performed

Removed missing and inconsistent values

Standardized date and time format

Created Peak Hour and Weekend flags

Generated Month and Day fields for trend analysis

# Dashboard Insights

1. Total Accidents KPI

Shows the overall number of recorded accidents, giving a quick overview of the dataset size and helping monitor accident volume.

2. Accidents by State

Insight: A small number of states contribute a significantly larger share of accidents.

Business Value: Helps government agencies prioritize road safety investments and enforcement in high-risk states.

3. Top 10 Cities by Accidents

Insight: Urban cities experience higher accident frequency due to dense traffic and population.

Business Value: Identifies cities where traffic management and infrastructure improvements are most needed.

4. Monthly Accident Trend

Insight: Accident numbers fluctuate across months, indicating seasonal or travel-related patterns.

Business Value: Supports planning for awareness campaigns during high-risk months.

5. Accident Severity Distribution

Insight: Most accidents fall into Minor and Moderate categories, while Severe accidents represent a smaller but critical portion.

Business Value: Helps emergency response teams allocate healthcare and rescue resources effectively.

6. Weather Impact Analysis

Insight: Rainy and foggy conditions are associated with increased accident occurrence because of reduced visibility and road grip.

Business Value: Enables authorities to issue weather-based travel alerts and improve road safety measures.

7. Peak Hour Analysis

Insight: Accidents increase during morning and evening commuting hours.

Business Value: Supports optimization of traffic signal timing and police deployment during rush hours.

8. Road Type Analysis

Insight: Highways and major roads experience a larger number of accidents due to higher vehicle speed.

Business Value: Highlights where speed monitoring and road engineering improvements should be prioritized.

# Business Solutions

High accidents in specific states : Targeted road safety programs and infrastructure investment

Urban traffic collisions : Smart traffic management and CCTV monitoring

Peak-hour accidents : Dynamic traffic signal optimization and police deployment

Weather-related accident : Real-time weather alerts and variable speed limits

Severe highway accident : Speed cameras, better lighting, and emergency response stations
